Lesson 6 · 14 min

Form as a tool: toolname and toolautosubmit (experimental, outside the spec)

Chrome lets a form become a tool with three HTML attributes. It's the shortest path for anyone who already has a well-labeled form, but it was left out of the 10/16/2026 spec: it exists in Chrome's origin trial and in a temporary layer of the polyfill. In this lesson you use it with that label and decide when the form can submit itself.

Start the lesson

In this lesson you will

  1. 1toolname, tooldescription, and toolparamdescription
  2. 2The schema the browser builds from the fields
  3. 3toolautosubmit only for what only reads
  4. 4agentInvoked and respondWith on submit

Goal: turn a form into a tool with toolname, knowing it's experimental and outside the spec, and decide when to use toolautosubmit.

01 · From form to tool

Experimental as of 10/19/2026, outside the spec. Click each step.

Use the imperative API (lessons 3 to 5) as your base. The declarative form is an extra: if it goes away, the form is still just a form.

WebMCP README: "Future Exploration… not part of the specification at this time". Chrome documents it and implements it in the trial (page updated on 09/25/2026).

02 · Two forms: one submits itself, the other waits

Excerpt from kit/pagina/declarativa.html. The search only reads and can use toolautosubmit; the booking has an effect and waits for the click.

Excerpt · kit/pagina/declarativa.html (HTML)
<!-- Só lê: pode enviar sozinho (toolautosubmit). -->
<form id="f-busca" toolname="buscar_oficinas_form" tooldescription="Busca oficinas da agenda por tema. Só lê." toolautosubmit>
  <label for="d-tema">Tema</label>
  <select id="d-tema" name="tema" required toolparamdescription="Tema da oficina.">
    <option value="html">HTML</option><option value="javascript">JavaScript</option>
    <option value="webmcp">WebMCP</option><option value="mcp">MCP</option>
  </select>
  <button type="submit">Buscar</button>
</form>

<!-- Tem efeito: SEM toolautosubmit. O agente preenche, a pessoa clica em Reservar. -->
<form id="f-reserva" toolname="reservar_vaga_form" tooldescription="Preenche a reserva de uma vaga. Quem envia é a pessoa, clicando em Reservar.">
  <label for="d-id">Oficina (id)</label>
  <input id="d-id" name="id" required pattern="of-[0-9]{3}" toolparamdescription="Id da oficina, ex.: of-301.">
  <label for="d-nome">Seu nome</label>
  <input id="d-nome" name="nome" required minlength="2">
  <button type="submit">Reservar</button>
</form>
Excerpt · kit/pagina/declarativa.html (submit)
$('#f-busca').addEventListener('submit', e => {
  e.preventDefault();
  const lista = agenda.buscar({ tema: $('#d-tema').value });
  $('#d-resultados').replaceChildren(...lista.map(o => Object.assign(document.createElement('li'), { textContent: `${o.id} · ${o.titulo}` })));
  if (e.agentInvoked) e.respondWith(Promise.resolve({ total: lista.length, ids: lista.map(o => o.id) }));
});

The search returns right away (toolautosubmit). The booking stays filled in and paused until the click: declarativa.mjs plays the part of the person.

With toolautosubmit on the booking form, the agent fills it in and submits it without the person seeing. Save automatic submission for what only reads. And remember: respondWith requires preventDefault() first, otherwise the browser goes ahead with the normal submission.

05 · In three sentences

Outside the spectoolname/tooldescription/toolautosubmit exist in Chrome in trial, not in the 10/16/2026 spec.
Who submitstoolautosubmit only on what only reads; everything else waits for the person's click.
